HOW TO ORDER

Bring the file.
We’ll handle the print.

Use the website to prepare the job before contacting the New Kingston team. Final specifications, artwork suitability, stock and turnaround are confirmed by HOTP.

01

Prepare your artwork

For business cards, HOTP’s guide specifies a standard 3.5 × 2 inch setup, preferably with bleed and printer’s crop marks.

02

Choose the production details

Tell us quantity, size, colour, stock or material, finishing and whether design/layout assistance is required.

03

Confirm the quote

Published rates are a starting point. HOTP confirms the final job cost, taxes, artwork/setup and any special production requirements.

04

Production & collection

Once the job is confirmed, coordinate production and collection with the team at Shops #36–39, New Kingston Business Centre.
