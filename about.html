HOTP describes itself as Jamaica’s largest one-stop desktop printer, combining an experienced team, in-house design support, laser printing, large format and finishing under one operation.

THE HOTP STORY

Printing Jamaica
since 1996.

More than a counter-service print shop, HOTP has built a production operation around speed, range and experienced people.

Established in 1996, HOTP says it has grown into Jamaica’s largest one-stop desktop printer. Its team includes more than 40 staff members and over 15 graphic artists, with the business emphasizing timely, professional service and top-quality output.

The production range covers colour and black-and-white laser printing, customized design, invitations, programmes, business cards, certificates, menus, bookmarkers, press kits, posters, banners and more.
